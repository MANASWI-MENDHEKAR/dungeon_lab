## 🎮 What Is This?

You build a dungeon. AI scientists lose their minds about it.

- 🧱 **Place tiles** — lava pits, goblin nests, boss chambers, spike traps, teapot rooms...
- 📊 **Watch absurd live metrics** — Hero Mortality Probability, Goblin Wellbeing Index, Lava Carbon Footprint, Evil Density (evil/ft²)
- 🔬 **Get roasted in real time** — three AI-powered academic reviewers react to every tile you place with pompous, unhinged scientific commentary
- 📜 **Submit for Peer Review** — receive a full fake academic paper with DOI, abstract, methodology critique, and a verdict stamp

**It solves zero real-world problems. That is the point.**

---

## 🕹️ How To Play

1. **Click a tile** from the left panel to select it
2. **Click or drag** on the grid to paint your dungeon
3. Watch the **right panel metrics** explode in real time
4. Hit **📜 SUBMIT FOR PEER REVIEW** with at least 3 tiles placed
5. Receive your fate — probably a **DESK REJECTION**

### 💡 Tips to get ACCEPTED
- Always start with an **Entrance** tile
- Add a **Boss Chamber** — reviewers take you more seriously
- Keep trap density above **2.7/room** (Tomb of Horrors standard, 1978)
- Add a **Magic Portal** for arcane complexity points
- Never place **Goblins next to Lava** — Dr. Skullvane will file a formal complaint
